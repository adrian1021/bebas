Your financial freedom plan

Financial freedom is when your investments can pay your living costs. Trading can speed this up, but saving and long-term investing do most of the work.

Where trading fits

  • Core first. Emergency fund (6 months of costs), then steady long-term investing (EPF, ASB, low-cost index or dividend funds). This is the engine.
  • Trading account is a satellite. Start with money you can afford to lose, say 5 to 10% of your investments, until your journal shows 50+ trades with positive expectancy.
  • Scale only with evidence. Add capital to trading only after 6 months of following the rules with a positive average R.
  • Take profits home. Each quarter, move part of any trading profit into the core portfolio. That is how trading turns into freedom.
